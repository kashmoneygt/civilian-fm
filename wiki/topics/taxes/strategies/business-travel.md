# Business Travel Deductions for S-Corp Owners

Business travel deductions allow S-corp owners to reduce their taxable income by deducting expenses incurred while traveling for business purposes. Understanding what qualifies as an ordinary and necessary expense is crucial for maximizing these deductions.

## Key facts
- **Ordinary and Necessary**: Expenses must be "ordinary and necessary" for your business to qualify for deductions. This means they are common and accepted in your industry and helpful for your business operations [source: irs-gov--forms-pubs-about-publication-463.md].
- **Away-from-Home Rule**: To qualify for deductions, you must be traveling away from your tax home for longer than a normal workday and need to sleep or rest to meet the demands of your work [source: irs-gov--forms-pubs-about-publication-463.md].
- **Qualifying Expenses**: Deductible expenses include transportation (airfare, car rental), lodging, meals (50% deductible), and other necessary expenses incurred while traveling for business [source: irs-gov--forms-pubs-about-publication-463.md].
- **Record Keeping**: You must keep detailed records of your travel expenses, including receipts and a log of your business purpose for each trip [source: irs-gov--forms-pubs-about-publication-463.md].

## How it applies to an S-corp owner
- **Expense Reimbursements**: If your S-corp reimburses you for travel expenses, these reimbursements are generally not taxable to you as long as they are substantiated [source: irs-gov--forms-pubs-about-publication-463.md].
- **Deducting Meals**: While meals are deductible, only 50% of the cost can be claimed, so it's essential to track these expenses accurately [source: irs-gov--forms-pubs-about-publication-463.md].
- **Traveling with Family**: If you travel with family members, their expenses are not deductible unless they are employees of the business and the travel is necessary for business purposes [source: irs-gov--forms-pubs-about-publication-463.md].

## Limits and gotchas
- **Personal vs. Business Travel**: If a trip combines personal and business activities, only the expenses directly related to the business portion are deductible [source: irs-gov--forms-pubs-about-publication-463.md].
- **Luxury Water Travel**: Expenses for luxury water travel (e.g., cruises) are subject to stricter limitations and may not be fully deductible [source: irs-gov--forms-pubs-about-publication-463.md].
- **Documentation Requirements**: Failing to maintain adequate documentation can lead to disallowed deductions during an audit [source: irs-gov--forms-pubs-about-publication-463.md].
