# SEP-IRA for Small Businesses and S-Corps

A Simplified Employee Pension (SEP) plan allows small business owners, including S-corp owners, to make tax-deductible contributions to their employees' retirement accounts, providing a flexible and cost-effective way to save for retirement.

## Key facts
- **Contribution Limits**: Employers can contribute up to "25 percent of each employee's pay" to their SEP-IRAs, with a maximum limit of $66,000 for 2023 [source: irs-gov--retirement-plans-plan-sponsor-simplified-employee-pension-plan-sep.md].
- **Employer-Only Contributions**: Only employers can contribute to SEP-IRAs; employees cannot make contributions [source: irs-gov--retirement-plans-plan-sponsor-simplified-employee-pension-plan-sep.md].
- **No Filing Requirements**: Employers generally have no annual filing requirements for a SEP plan [source: irs-gov--retirement-plans-plan-sponsor-simplified-employee-pension-plan-sep.md].
- **100% Vesting**: Employees are always 100% vested in their SEP-IRA contributions [source: irs-gov--retirement-plans-plan-sponsor-simplified-employee-pension-plan-sep.md].
- **Flexible Contributions**: Contributions can vary each year based on the business's cash flow [source: irs-gov--retirement-plans-plan-sponsor-simplified-employee-pension-plan-sep.md].

## How it applies to an S-corp owner
- **Tax Deduction**: Contributions made by the S-corp to employee SEP-IRAs are tax-deductible, reducing the overall taxable income of the business [source: irs-gov--retirement-plans-plan-sponsor-simplified-employee-pension-plan-sep.md].
- **Retirement Savings**: S-corp owners can contribute to their own SEP-IRA, allowing them to save significantly for retirement while benefiting from tax deductions [source: irs-gov--retirement-plans-plan-sponsor-simplified-employee-pension-plan-sep.md].
- **No Employee Contributions**: Since only employer contributions are allowed, S-corp owners can control the amount contributed without employee involvement [source: irs-gov--retirement-plans-plan-sponsor-simplified-employee-pension-plan-sep.md].
- **Simplicity**: The SEP plan is easier to set up and maintain compared to other retirement plans, making it ideal for small businesses [source: irs-gov--retirement-plans-plan-sponsor-simplified-employee-pension-plan-sep.md].

## Limits and gotchas
- **Uniform Contributions**: Employers must contribute the same percentage for all eligible employees, which can limit flexibility in compensation strategies [source: irs-gov--retirement-plans-plan-sponsor-simplified-employee-pension-plan-sep.md].
- **Eligibility Requirements**: Employees must meet certain criteria (age, service duration, and compensation) to be eligible for contributions, which may exclude some workers [source: irs-gov--retirement-plans-plan-sponsor-simplified-employee-pension-plan-sep.md].
- **No Loans or In-Service Withdrawals**: Participants cannot take loans against their SEP-IRA, and in-service withdrawals are subject to taxes and penalties if taken before age 59½ [source: irs-gov--retirement-plans-plan-sponsor-simplified-employee-pension-plan-sep.md].
