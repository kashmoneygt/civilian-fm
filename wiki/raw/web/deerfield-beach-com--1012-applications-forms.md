# Applications & Forms

### EXPEDITED PROCESSING NOTICE FOR AFFORDABLE HOUSING PERMITS

If an applicant believes it is entitled to expedited processing of an application for a building permit, the applicant shall advise the City upon submission of the application of the statutory basis entitling it to expedited processing.   The City shall give applications for building permits or development orders that are entitled to expedited processing first priority and applicants may expect that such applications will be reviewed and processed quicker than other applications. Please note - all permit applications require the owner's signature in Deerfield Beach.
