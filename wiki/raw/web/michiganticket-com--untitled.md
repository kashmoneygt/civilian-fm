## Drug & Marijuana Offenses

Drug-related offenses are some of the most serious charges a driver can face. These charges can carry severe consequences, including hefty fines, license suspension, probation, and even imprisonment. For commercial drivers, a conviction can mean the loss of employment and future career opportunities.

## License Restoration

Driving in Michigan is considered a privilege. When a driver commits specific traffic violations or legal offenses, the Michigan Secretary of State (SOS) has the authority to suspend or revoke their driver’s license. This can significantly impact one’s ability to work, run errands, or maintain personal responsibilities.

## Drunk Driving

Drunk driving offenses are among the most complex and serious legal matters a driver can face. These charges come with severe penalties, including hefty fines, license suspension, mandatory alcohol education programs, and potential jail time.  A conviction for driving under the influence (DUI) or related offenses can have long-term consequences

## Speeding Tickets

If you have been cited for a traffic violation in Michigan, whether it’s a minor speeding ticket or a serious reckless driving charge, you may be facing significant consequences that extend beyond just paying a fine. Your insurance premiums, driving record, and even your ability to retain your driver’s license can all be affected.

## Distracted Driving Tickets

Distracted driving laws are getting stricter, and the consequences of using a handheld device, texting while driving, or any form of distracted driving can be severe. Whether you were caught holding your phone at a red light or texting while stuck in traffic, a single ticket can result in heavy fines, points on your license, increased insurance rates, and even license suspension for repeat offenses.

## Overweight CMV Tickets

If you’ve received an overweight ticket for exceeding weight limits on a commercial vehicle, the consequences can go beyond a simple fine. Overweight violations can lead to hefty penalties, DOT compliance issues, damage to your safety score, and even vehicle impoundment—all of which can hurt your trucking business and your ability to stay on the road.

## Log Book & Duty Hours Violations

For commercial drivers, keeping an accurate logbook and adhering to Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) regulations is essential to staying compliant and avoiding serious penalties. However, logbook violations and duty hour infractions remain some of the most common offenses for CDL holders and trucking companies.

## Careless or Reckless Driving

Driving violations are categorized based on the level of danger they pose to others on the road. Among the most serious offenses are careless and reckless driving, both of which can lead to fines, license points, insurance hikes, and even license suspension. However, many drivers are unaware of the differences between these two violations and how they impact their driving records.

## Equipment Violations

Maintaining a safe and legally compliant vehicle is a crucial responsibility for all drivers, particularly those operating commercial vehicles. Equipment violations, such as faulty brakes, broken lights, worn-out tires, and other safety defects, can lead to steep fines, vehicle impoundment, license points, and even commercial driving disqualification.
