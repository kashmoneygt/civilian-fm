# Mountlake Terrace

Whether you’re traveling to your local grocery store or commuting to Seattle , Community Transit can get you around Mountlake Terrace without a car. Our local buses and Swift bus rapid transit operate seven days a week, and you can pay for these services with your ORCA card . Finally, Sound Transit buses and Link light rail can take you from Mountlake Terrace to Seattle or the Eastside.

### Link light rail

Community Transit buses can take you to Mountlake Terrace Station , where two Link lines stop. The 1 Line travels south to Seattle, Sea-Tac Airport, and Federal Way, while the 2 Line crosses Lake Washington to Bellevue and ends in Redmond.
