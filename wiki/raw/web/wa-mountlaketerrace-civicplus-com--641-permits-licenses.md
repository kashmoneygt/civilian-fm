## Typical Building Permit Timelines

### Over the Counter: Same day

### Residential: 4-6 weeks

### Commercial: 6-8 weeks

### Revisions: 2 weeks

Homeowners considering a remodel to their home, or adding improvements such as decks, roofs, garages or retaining walls, often have questions about building permits. Because each construction project is unique, we invite you to refer to our FAQ page , or contact a Permit Specialist via the Ask A Permit Specialist Form to answer your specific permitting questions. Permit Specialists can be reached at permitspecialist@mltwa.gov .

Inspections: Those with active permits can schedule an inspection via the online Permit Portal .

Your project must have issued permits on-site.
