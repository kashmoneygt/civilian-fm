## mountlake terrace

Residents of this new development will enjoy easy access to downtown Seattle and other major employment centers in the region, thanks to the proximity of the Mountlake Terrace Light Rail Station. The location also offers convenient access to several local shops, restaurants, schools, and parks, making it the perfect place for those who want a quieter suburban setting with easy access to urban amenities.

The project is thoughtfully designed to offer a comfortable and convenient lifestyle, with a beautifully landscaped private courtyard featuring grilling stations, a bocce ball court, and a kid-friendly play area. Additional quality amenities include a fully equipped fitness center and yoga room, a large Clubroom kitchen with dining areas, a lounge with billiards, arcade and foosball, and a covered rooftop deck with stunning views of the Cascade and Olympic Mountains.

The project is currently in the process of obtaining its land use permit, with the building permit application set to vest in June. Construction is expected to begin as early as Q1 2026, pending permit approvals. In addition to providing much-needed housing and retail space, this new mixed-use project promises to breathe new life into the vibrant Town Center area, energizing this active community. With its prime location and modern amenities, this project is an ideal place to call home for those seeking both comfort and convenience.
