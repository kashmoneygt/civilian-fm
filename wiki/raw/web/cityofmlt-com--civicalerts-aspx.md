# NEWS: The City of Mountlake Terrace Announces New Mayor

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE January 12, 2026

## The City of Mountlake Terrace Announces New Mayor Newly-Elected Councilmembers Sworn in at January 8 th Council Meeting

(Mountlake Terrace, WA) -- On January 8, a new mayor was elected by the Mountlake Terrace City Council and four newly-elected councilmembers were sworn in following the November 4 election.

Dr. Steve Woodard (Mayor / Position 2) Bryan Wahl (Mayor Pro Tem / Position 5) Councilmember Sam Doyle (Position 1) Councilmember William L. Paige, Jr. (Position 3) Councilmember Kyoko Matsumoto Wright (Position 4) Councilmember Laura Sonmore (Position 6) Councilmember Erin Murray (Position 7)

“Thank you to our Council and residents, including the staff, of our beautiful destination City of Mountlake Terrace for your ongoing trust. My goal is to continue to make you all proud of your thoughtful decision to elect me as Councilmember and now appoint me as Mayor,” said Mayor Steve Woodard. Dr. Woodard has served as a Mountlake Terrace Councilmember since 2019 and is Vice President of Volunteers of America, Western Washington.

In addition, the City announces the 2026 Intergovernmental Board Appointments:

MLT Arts Advisory Commission: William Paige MLT Board and Commission Interview Subcommittee: Sam Doyle, Erin Murray, William Paige MLT Diversity, Equity & Inclusion Commission: Erin Murray MLT Finance Committee: Bryan Wahl, Steve Woodard MLT LEOFF 1 Disability Board: Laura Sonmore, Steve Woodard MLT Lodging Tax Advisory Committee: Laura Sonmore MLT Personnel (City Manager) Review Subcommittee: Sam Doyle, Laura Sonmore MLT Recreation and Park Advisory Commission: Laura Sonmore Alliance for Housing Affordability (AHA) Board: Primary: Kyoko Matsumoto Wright / Alt: Bryan Wahl Lake Ballinger/McAleer Creek Watershed Forum: Primary: Erin Murray / Alt: Steve Woodard SeaShore Transportation Forum: Primary: Steve Woodard / Alt: Erin Murray Snohomish County Tomorrow (SCT) Steering Committee: Primary: Bryan Wahl / Alt: Steve Woodard

City Council Meetings take place the first four Thursdays of the month @ 7 p.m. at City Hall. Regular Council Meetings are held on the 1st and 3rd Thursdays; Council Work Sessions are held on the 2nd and 4th Thursdays. Council Meetings are available to watch every week on our YouTube channel: @cityofmlt.

City Council schedule, agendas, and recordings are available on our website: cityofmlt.com; click on “Council Meetings.”
