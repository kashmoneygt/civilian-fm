## About Mountlake Terrace High School

Mountlake Terrace High School, established in 1960, is a public high school in Mountlake Terrace, Washington, serving approximately 1,350 students in grades 9-12. As part of the Edmonds School District, it is the third largest high school in the district. The school's mascot is the Hawks, and its colors are black, red, scarlet, and silver. Mountlake Terrace High School is renowned for its exceptional Jazz Band and successful Basketball program, which has won the Wesco Championship eight times.
