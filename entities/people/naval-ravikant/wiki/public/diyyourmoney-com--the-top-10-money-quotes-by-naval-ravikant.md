### Who is Naval Ravikant?

Naval Ravikant is an Indian-American entrepreneur, investor, and author. He is the co-founder and chairman of AngelList, a platform for startups to raise money from angel investors. He is also the author of the book “The Almanack of Naval Ravikant: A Guide to Wealth, Wisdom, and Success.”
