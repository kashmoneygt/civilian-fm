## Frequently Asked Questions about Heritage Tree Care

### Are you licensed and insured in Washington State?

Yes, we are fully licensed, bonded, and insured in Washington State with $2 million liability coverage. Our ISA certified arborists meet all state requirements and carry comprehensive worker's compensation protection.

### Do you offer 24/7 emergency tree service?

Yes, we provide 24/7 emergency response throughout Mountlake Terrace and Snohomish County. Our emergency crews typically respond within 1-2 hours for hazardous tree situations, storm damage, and fallen trees.

### Do you provide free estimates for tree services?

Yes, we provide free estimates for all tree services. Our certified arborists assess your trees on-site and provide detailed written quotes with transparent pricing and no hidden fees.

### What areas do you serve?

We serve Mountlake Terrace, Lynnwood, Edmonds, Shoreline, Brier, Lake Forest Park, and surrounding Snohomish County communities within a 25-mile radius.

### Is cleanup included with tree services?

Yes, complete site cleanup is included with every tree service. We remove all debris, branches, and wood from your property, haul everything away, and leave your yard clean at no additional charge.

### Can you safely work near power lines?

Yes, our certified arborists are trained to work safely around power lines. We follow strict utility clearance protocols and coordinate with power companies when necessary to ensure safe operations.

### What qualifies as a heritage tree in Mountlake Terrace?

Heritage trees typically meet specific size criteria (diameter and height thresholds) or are designated historically significant species. Mountlake Terrace maintains a registry of protected trees subject to special regulations.

### Do I need a permit to work on a heritage tree?

Yes, most work on heritage trees requires permits including removal, major pruning, or construction within the tree protection zone. We handle all permit applications and required documentation.

### How long does heritage tree permit approval take?

Permit processing typically takes 2-6 weeks depending on application completeness and city review workload. Emergency situations may receive expedited review for hazardous trees.

### What maintenance is required for heritage trees?

Heritage trees require regular monitoring, proper pruning following approved plans, protection during construction, and compliance with city ordinances. We provide ongoing maintenance programs meeting all regulatory requirements.
