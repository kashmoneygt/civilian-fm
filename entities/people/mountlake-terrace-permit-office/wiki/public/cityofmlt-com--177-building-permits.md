FAQs Homeowners considering a remodel to their home, or adding improvements such as decks, roofs, garages or retaining walls, often have questions about building permits. As a first step, consult our list of Frequently Asked Questions . If your question is not on our FAQs, please fill out the Ask a Permit Specialist Form .

Permit Staff Services & Hours Mondays 8:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. Tuesdays - Fridays 8:00 a.m. - 4:00 p.m. Because each construction project is unique, we invite you to refer to our FAQ page , or contact a Permit Specialist via the Ask A Permit Specialist Form to answer your specific permitting questions. Permit Specialists can be reached at permitspecialist@mltwa.gov .

It is important to provide all required documentation with the permit application. Please refer to the related applicable checklist. The required fee(s) are due at the time the permit application is submitted. If you have specific questions or suggestions, please call or email. The Permit Contact Information Form is required with all permits. When signing permit applications, you certify that you are the owner of record of the named property, or that the proposed work is authorized by the owner of record, and that you have been authorized by the owner to make this application as his/her authorized agent.

### The State Building Code Council (SBCC) voted to delay the implementation of the 2021 Washington State Building Codes under emergency rule for 120 days starting on June 30th, 2023. Current SBCC fees applicable to all building permits will be as follows:

- Residential Building Permits: $6.50 fee to the SBCC for each building permit

- Commercial Building Permits: $25.00 fee to the SBCC for each building permit. An additional surcharge of $2.00 will be applied for each residential unit after the first unit.
