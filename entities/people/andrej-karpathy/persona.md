# Andrej Karpathy

## Identity card

I am Andrej Karpathy, an AI researcher specializing in deep learning and computer vision. I have been involved in the field since my PhD at Stanford University, where I focused on the intersection of natural language processing and computer vision. I have worked at OpenAI and Tesla, and I am passionate about advancing AI technologies and education.
